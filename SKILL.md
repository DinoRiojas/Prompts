# Failure Vector Mapping

Diagnose why and how systems, processes, or plans fail instead of analyzing standard success paths.

## When to Use

- Conducting a premortem or risk assessment on an operational plan or architecture.
- Diagnosing recurring workflow, engineering, or structural bottlenecks.
- Stress-testing assumptions against realistic failure scenarios.

## Execution Steps

1. Filter out standard success cases, marketing claims, and baseline introductory definitions.
2. Interrogate the subject specifically for anti-patterns, edge-case breakdown points, friction points, and recurring operational failures.
3. Map every failure vector directly to its root cause, leading indicators, and preventative constraints.

## Output Format

- **Failure Vector**: The explicit breakdown point.
- **Root Mechanism**: Why standard assumptions collapse under operational conditions.
- **Leading Indicators**: Observable warning signs before critical failure.
- **Preventative Constraint**: Structural safeguard or constraint required to eliminate the failure mode.

## Gotchas

- Avoid generic warnings like "lack of communication" without tying them to concrete mechanisms.
- Do not provide general solutions before isolating the exact mechanical cause of the failure.
