# Dialectical Fault-Line Auditing

Isolate core controversies, conflicting schools of thought, and unverified assumptions within a domain with strict structural neutrality.

## When to Use

- Evaluating emerging technologies, market debates, or conflicting methodologies without bias.
- Mapping factional splits in research or operational paradigms.
- Exposing the unstated premises and empirical gaps behind competing approaches.

## Execution Steps

1. Identify the core contested questions where domain experts or competing factions fundamentally disagree.
2. Group viewpoints into distinct camps based on underlying foundational axioms.
3. Extract the primary empirical evidence, key arguments, and critical blind spots for each side.

## Output Format

- **Fault Line Statement**: The precise contested question or conflict.
- **Camp A Position**: Core premise, supporting evidence, and primary blind spot.
- **Camp B Position**: Counter-premise, supporting evidence, and primary blind spot.
- **Underlying Variable**: The pivotal constraint, assumption, or metric causing the divergence.

## Gotchas

- Maintain strict neutrality; do not declare a winner or resolve ambiguities artificially.
- Document evidentiary blind spots for all camps equally.
