# Tacit Heuristics Extraction

Surface operational nuances, unwritten rules, and real-world tradecraft that are omitted from formal manuals and textbooks.

## When to Use

- Bridging the gap between formal theory and frontline practitioner execution.
- Extracting operational shortcuts and informal heuristics from complex workflows.
- Auditing real-world execution practices against academic or textbook protocols.

## Execution Steps

1. Scan the domain or materials for implicit assumptions, operational workarounds, and informal field adaptations.
2. Contrast formal textbook methodologies directly against actual field practices.
3. Strip away high-level platitudes to retain high-density, context-specific decision rules.

## Output Format

- **Documented Rule vs. Field Reality**: Direct contrast between official doctrine and actual practice.
- **Heuristic Formula**: Actionable "If [Scenario X], then [Action Y]" operational rule.
- **Trade-Off Assessment**: Explicit cost, risk, or technical debt incurred by adopting the shortcut.

## Gotchas

- Ensure heuristics include operational boundaries; an unconstrained shortcut leads to reckless execution.
- Avoid restating standard best practices as "secret" heuristics.
