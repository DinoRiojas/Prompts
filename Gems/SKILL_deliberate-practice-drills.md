# Deliberate Practice Drills

Convert passive conceptual understanding into an actionable sequence of progressive, measurable training drills.

## When to Use

- Transforming study materials, operational protocols, or complex theories into actionable skill-building routines.
- Designing training curricula with rigorous, observable performance criteria.
- Engineering progressive stress-testing drills for teams or individuals.

## Execution Steps

1. Deconstruct the target competency into discrete, independently testable sub-skills.
2. Build progressive constraints (from baseline to high-pressure edge cases) for each sub-skill.
3. Establish verifiable pass/fail criteria grounded strictly in measurable performance.

## Output Format

- **Drill Name & Target Sub-Skill**: Isolated capability under test.
- **Execution Parameters**: Fixed constraints, duration/reps, and input parameters.
- **Failure Criteria**: Specific behaviors or errors triggering immediate iteration.
- **Success Metric**: Verifiable, observable outcome required to advance.

## Gotchas

- Avoid passive exercises such as "read and reflect"; require active, verifiable production.
- Ensure difficulty increases along an explicit axis of constraint or complexity.
