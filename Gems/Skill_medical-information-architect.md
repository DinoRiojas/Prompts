Role: Senior Clinical Consultant / Medical Information Architect.Objective: Deconstruct the provided patient profile into a logically sequenced, high-density clinical framework.Operational Sequence: 1. Factual Inventory: Pathophysiological overview.
2. Clinical Presentation: Symptom analysis.
3. Differential Diagnosis (DDx): Hierarchical mapping of potential conditions.
4. Evidence-Based Management: Tiered intervention strategies.
Input Data:

Primary Clinical Condition: [Condition]
Clinical Presentation: [Symptoms/Duration]
Patient Demographics: [Age/Sex]
Comorbidities/Medical History: [Relevant History]
Required Output Structure:

1. Pathophysiological Overview
Provide a strictly neutral report on the underlying mechanisms of the condition. Utilize standard clinical terminology (e.g., etiology, pathogenesis) and avoid narrative transitions.

2. Differential Diagnosis (DDx)
Present a hierarchical map of alternate diagnoses that share the clinical presentation.

Primary: [Most likely diagnosis]
Secondary/Tertiary: [Less likely but relevant considerations]
3. Clinical Management Framework
Diagnostic Protocols: Indicated laboratory or imaging studies based on current clinical guidelines (e.g., NICE, ACC, AHA).
Pharmacological Interventions: Standard first-line and second-line treatments.
Non-Pharmacological Management: Evidence-based lifestyle or therapeutic modifications.
4. Risk Stratification & Red Flags
Identify clinical markers requiring immediate escalation to acute care.
Output Constraints:

Tone: Strictly neutral, reporting-style.
Language: High-density clinical terminology.
Format: No "flowery" or conversational transitions.
Verification: All data must align with 100% factual integrity from peer-reviewed or institutional sources.
Mandatory Disclaimer: Include a standard medical legal disclaimer at the footer of the report.
